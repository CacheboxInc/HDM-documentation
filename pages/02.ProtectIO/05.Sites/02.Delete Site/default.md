You can delete a protected site by following below steps

>>>>> If you delete a protected site, then the VMs on the site are no longer be protected by the ProtectIO.

##### **Procedure**

+ From the left navigation, select **Sites**. You will see the list of Primary and DR sites on the right side.
+ From available actions option, click **Delete**.
+ Click "YES" on the confirmation dialogue box to delete the site.
