Edit CDP policy to provide different name, add or remove VMs or choose different DR site.

>>>>> Virtual machines must be in powered on state for Continuous Data Replication

##### **Procedure**
+ From the left navigation, select **Primary Site**.
+ On the right side, select **CDP Configuration** tab.
+ Click on **EDIT** button which will open a dialogue box.
+ On first page, you can edit the **Name** of the CDP policy and **Description**.
+ When you click **Next**, you can also add or remove virtual machines that you want to protect.
+ Next, choose the **DR site** from the drop down menu.
+ Next, verify the details and click **UPDATE**.
