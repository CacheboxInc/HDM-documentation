! Updated on 05/16/2023

Before PrimaryIO works on onboarding steps, customer needs to subscribe to ProtectIO service. There are two ways to do this:

1. **Via IBM Cloud Tile**

	+ ProtectIO tile is present in IBM Cloud catalog
	+ Login to [IBM cloud](https://cloud.ibm.com/)



2. **Via email id**
+ Send an email to PrimaryIO at [engage@primaryio.com](engage@primaryio.com). PrimaryIO team will contact you and help you with further steps
